A kit plugin for Paper and Spigot. Every player builds their own kits, from the items you approve.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/MainMenu.png" alt="The PerPlayerKit main menu, showing nine kit slots" width="340" />
</Frame>

One command fills the kit room and nine public kits with a ready-made setup, so a new server is playable immediately.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stock the kit room">
    Add the items players are allowed to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Players build kits">
    A player runs `/kit`, arranges an inventory, and saves it as a kit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Players load kits">
    `/k1` loads kit 1, `/k2` loads kit 2. No menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

| Feature | Does |
| ------- | ---- |
| [Kits](/features/kits) | Nine kits and nine enderchest kits per player, up to 99. |
| [Public kits](/features/public-kits) | Server-wide preset kits, loaded with `/publickit`. |
| [Kit sharing](/features/sharing) | Send a kit to a player, give out a share code, or move every kit to a new account. |
| [Automatic rekit](/features/rekit) | A kit back on respawn, or a kit for the killer after every kill. |
| [Regear](/features/regear) | Restock pearls, crystals, and potions mid-fight from the loaded kit. |
| [Anti-exploit](/features/anti-exploit) | Blocks illegal enchantments and <Tooltip tip="Hidden data attached to an item, such as its enchantments or custom name.">NBT</Tooltip> data. Kits can be limited to kit room items only. |

## Get started

Requires Paper or Spigot **1.19 or newer** and **Java 17 or newer**.
